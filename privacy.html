Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Clipfarm is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its operator to post videos to the operator's own TikTok account.

What we access

When the operator authorises Clipfarm with TikTok, TikTok provides an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name). Clipfarm uses these only to upload videos to that same account.

What we store

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access makes stored tokens useless; the operator can also delete them locally.
